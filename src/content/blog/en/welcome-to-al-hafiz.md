## A store built on authenticity

Al Hafiz Nur Al-Madinah began with a simple idea: bring the authentic goods of
the Middle East closer to the community in Puncak Alam. From long-lasting Arabic
perfumes to hand-selected dates, everything on our shelves is chosen for quality.

## What you'll find with us

- **Arabic perfumes** — attars and concentrated oils with genuine Middle Eastern
  character.
- **Premium dates** — including the renowned Ajwa and Safawi varieties.
- **Middle Eastern foods** — imported spices, honey, and specialty pantry goods.
- **Islamic essentials** — everyday items for worship and gifting.

## Visit us

We'd love to welcome you in person. Drop by the store in Eco Grandeur, or reach
out on WhatsApp if you have a question before visiting.
