## Why Ajwa is special

Ajwa dates from Madinah are prized for their soft texture and balanced, mild
sweetness. Good Ajwa should feel tender rather than dry, with a wrinkled but not
brittle skin.

## What to look for

- **Texture:** soft and moist, never hard or crumbly.
- **Colour:** deep dark brown, close to black.
- **Aroma:** subtly sweet, without any fermented or sour note.

## Storing them well

Keep dates in an airtight container. At room temperature they'll stay good for
weeks; refrigerated, they last much longer while keeping their softness.

Come by the store to taste the difference before you buy.
